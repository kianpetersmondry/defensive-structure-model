PPDA vs. Phase Detector

MOROCCO vs SPAIN · 2022 World Cup R16 · Education City Stadium · Game 10508

Does the geometric phase detector (back-line depth + inter-line compactness) track the same story as PPDA, an independent pressing-intensity measure built from event data? Both are plotted here on the same true match clock — periodElapsedTime plus period-start offsets, not the frame-count-derived video_t the raw pickle carries, which runs up to ~12.5 minutes ahead of true playing time by extra time (see the note below the chart).

Full match, one clock

Both rows share one 5-minute bucket grid. Top: PPDA per team per bucket (defending team's opponent-passes-allowed per defensive action, ≥35m from their own goal). Bottom: each team's dominant organized-defense phase for that same bucket — High Press, Mid Block, or Low Block, whichever held for the most time among a team's own non-possession, non-transition frames inside it (excluding "on the ball" and "transition" from the vote on purpose: a team is on the ball more than it defends in any 5 minutes, so leaving those in would let them win almost every bucket by default and bury the actual defensive shape). A gap in a strip means that bucket had no qualifying organized-defense time at all, not a zero-th phase. Every block sits directly under its matching PPDA point. Hover to read both at once.

PPDA by dominant phase

Re-checking an open question from the project methodology: cross-tabulating PPDA by depth-based structure looked inverted for Morocco under the original whole-team-average depth metric. Each team's 5-minute buckets are grouped by whichever organized-defense phase (High Press / Mid Block / Low Block) held for most of that window — on-the-ball and transition time excluded from that vote, same as the chart above — and matched to that same bucket's PPDA.

Source: PFF FC World Cup 2022, game 10508 — Morocco vs Spain, Round of 16. Phase classification: back-line depth (40m/32m cutoffs) + inter-line compactness gate (20m). PPDA: opponent passes allowed per defensive action, press zone ≥35m from own goal, recomputed here on the true match clock (see note above).
