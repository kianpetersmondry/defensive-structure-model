Phase-detection definition · idea #1

Engaged vs. Passive Defense

Same geometric phases (High Press / Mid Block / Low Block) as the rest of Pressure Read — but each phase instance is also labeled by whether the team was actually closing someone down at the time, or just holding its line. A deep back line and a genuine low block aren't the same thing unless there's real engagement behind it.

Each block is one continuous phase instance (≥5s, gaps ≤3s merged — the same segmentation the project's own PPDA-by-structure validation uses), colored by geometric phase. Solid = Engaged: pressure_score (the time-to-intercept metric already used for the pressure meter) was at or above — for most of that instance's tracked frames. Diagonal hatch = Passive: the team held that phase's depth/compactness without real 1v1 pressure being applied. Muted grey = not enough resolved pressure data in that instance to call it either way.
