Defensive Structure Monitor

Renders a full match and reads each team's defensive structure whenever they're out of possession — where the space and gaps are, what's working, and how it could be exploited. Live phase classification, a pressure-on-ball-carrier score, an inter-line band, and a danger heatmap, all updating frame by frame.

Each match is split into ~5-minute chapters aligned to period breaks — a full match at full quality runs past what a single page can hold. Every chapter links to the next and previous one so you can keep watching straight through. Ball tracked is the share of that chapter's frames with a real tracked ball position; broadcast tracking has real gaps, so it varies chapter to chapter. Phase-detection definition lets you pick which version of the classification logic is driving the High Press / Mid Block / Low Block / Transition calls — useful for comparing an earlier definition against a later one once more than one exists.

Behind the Model

The research and checks that went into building the model. Scoped to the test case, Morocco vs Spain, only.
